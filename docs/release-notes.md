# Subtrack 0.1.0

Know what renews next, without connecting your bank or inbox.

- Add subscriptions and one-time access manually.
- Browse renewals in the calendar and agenda.
- Find services by name, plan, or category; sort by renewal date or name.
- See monthly and annual recurring-spend equivalents.
- Track trials, paused services, cancellations, and retained history.
- Use your preferred time zone, currency, and regional format.

## Install

Choose the signed installer for your computer: Apple silicon Mac, Intel Mac, or Windows x64. The release must include tested OS versions and SHA-256 checksums before publication. See the [user guide](https://github.com/UtkarshDubeyGIT/subtrack/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md).

## Before you start

Subtrack requires an online account and internet connection. It stores the subscription details you enter; it does not cancel services or change provider billing. Due reminders require online scheduling and the app to be open. Email/background reminders, automatic FX ingestion, account export/deletion, and automatic updates are unavailable. Missing exchange rates are excluded from totals.
